FAQ & Help Center

Answers about the X55 AI Telegram service, privacy, safety and legal policies.

Service

Do I need to install another application?

No. X55 AI works through Telegram.

Can I search worldwide?

You can configure your search area and discover people beyond your immediate location.

Will other users see my exact location?

No. The service is designed not to display your exact geographic position to other users.

Are Secret Desire entries public?

No. Secret Desire information is intended to remain private and is used for compatible matching rather than public profile display.

Privacy and AI processing

Does X55 process my messages with AI?

Yes. The current bot implementation uses AI-assisted processing for certain functions. User messages and private matching information may be processed by an external AI provider where required for the relevant feature. See the Privacy Policy.

Which external AI provider is currently used?

The current bot implementation uses DeepSeek for AI processing. The production Privacy Policy should be kept updated if the provider changes.

Does X55 sell my personal data?

No. X55 does not sell personal data. Information may be processed by technical service providers where necessary to operate the service, including Telegram, Stripe and external AI providers.

Safety

Is X55 available to people under 18?

No. X55 is an adults-only service. Persons under 18 may not use it.

Can X55 be used to arrange paid sexual services or prostitution?

No. Prostitution, commercial sexual services, trafficking, exploitation, grooming and child sexual exploitation are prohibited. See the Acceptable Use Policy.

How do I report abuse or suspected illegal activity?

Contact info@x55.ai or @X55AI. Include the relevant Telegram username or identifier, dates and a concise description where possible. For immediate danger, contact the appropriate emergency or law-enforcement service directly.
