Law Enforcement & Legal Requests Protocol

Effective date: 26 September 2026

1. Purpose

This protocol explains how X55 AI handles requests from law-enforcement agencies, courts and other competent governmental authorities seeking information or preservation of information.

2. Official requests

Requests should use an official governmental or law-enforcement channel and identify the authority, official contact, legal basis, jurisdiction, relevant account or identifier, categories of information requested and relevant period.

3. Validity and scope

X55 reviews requests for legal validity and scope and may seek clarification of incomplete, vague or overly broad requests. Only information legally required or lawfully permitted to be disclosed will be provided.

4. Preservation

Where applicable law permits or requires preservation, X55 may preserve information within its control that is reasonably identified by a valid request.

5. Emergencies

Requests involving an imminent risk of death or serious physical harm may be submitted through an official emergency or law-enforcement channel. X55 will evaluate them under applicable law.

6. Child safety and exploitation

Requests concerning suspected child sexual exploitation, trafficking, grooming or other serious abuse will be treated as high-priority safety matters and may be handled with competent authorities as required or permitted by law.

7. Potentially available information

Depending on the system and relevant period, information may include Telegram identifiers, profile data supplied to X55, service interaction information, search and matching information, payment references and technical records actually retained. X55 does not guarantee that any particular data exists or is retained for a particular period.

8. User notification

Where legally permitted, X55 may notify an affected user. Notification may be withheld where prohibited by law or where it could compromise an investigation.

9. Contact

Legal contact: Telegram @x55_ai

10. Scope

This protocol does not create an obligation to disclose information beyond what applicable law requires or permits.
